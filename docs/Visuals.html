SECTION 2: AI-DRIVEN DISCOVERY FRAMEWORK

The Structure-Based Machine Learning Drug Discovery Pipeline

Methodology Narrative

We designed a novel AI-driven drug discovery framework to identify compounds capable of reversing the C4A-associated transcriptional signature implicated in schizophrenia. The pipeline integrates multi-cohort transcriptomic analysis with a structure-based machine learning model that predicts molecular reversal potential directly from chemical structure.

First, a robust C4A gene signature was derived by integrating four independent schizophrenia transcriptomic datasets (GSE21138, GSE53987, GSE38484, GSE38485) spanning both brain and peripheral tissues. Data were standardized using RMA/quantile normalization and covariate correction, followed by cross-cohort correlation analysis to identify genes consistently co-varying with C4A (FDR < 0.05). The final upregulated and downregulated gene sets captured the core synaptic–immune dysregulation associated with C4A overexpression.

Next, we constructed a classification-based predictive model that learns the molecular determinants of signature reversal. The model was trained on compounds with known transcriptomic responses, labeled according to their connectivity with the C4A signature (reversing vs. non-reversing). Chemical structures were represented as graphs derived from SMILES strings, allowing the model to infer structural patterns predictive of transcriptomic reversal without requiring explicit molecular descriptors.

Once trained, the model was applied to a broad chemical library to predict novel compounds with high likelihood of reversing the C4A signature. These predictions were systematically annotated using PubChem and ChEMBL to extract pharmacological targets and pathway information. Several top candidates (e.g., BRD-K26684619, panobinostat, calcitriol) exhibited mechanistic coherence with C4A-linked immune and synaptic pathways, supporting the model’s biological validity.

This framework represents a new computational paradigm for drug discovery — an end-to-end, ML-driven system that infers therapeutic potential directly from molecular structure and disease-specific transcriptomic context, without relying on traditional docking or empirical screening.
